Technical Details

About HeartAI

Data Science Pipeline

Production-grade scikit-learn pipeline featuring median imputation, StandardScaler normalization, SMOTE oversampling for class imbalance, and PCA reduction to 20 components.

Model Performance

Logistic Regression achieves 83% test accuracy and 91% ROC-AUC. SMOTE rebalancing ensures equal sensitivity across disease states in the training set.

Clinical Biomarkers

10 mandatory inputs derived from hemodynamic monitoring: heart rate statistics, cardiac index, mean arterial pressure, and HRV spectral analysis (LF, HF, LF/HF).

Tech Stack

Backend: Python + Flask REST API. ML: Scikit-learn + imbalanced-learn. Frontend: Pure HTML/CSS/JS. Fully responsive, glassmorphism dark-themed architecture.
